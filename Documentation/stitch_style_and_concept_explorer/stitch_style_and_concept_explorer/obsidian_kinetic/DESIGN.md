## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-performance quiet luxury for serious athletic telemetry. It sits at the intersection of haute horlogerie, Swiss industrial precision, and disciplined human performance.

### Aesthetic Movement
A synthesis of **Dark-Mode Luxury Minimalism** and **Precision Instrument Instrumentation**. The visual environment rejects the common tropes of fitness apps—harsh neon floods, gamified badges, and noisy dopamine traps—in favor of a restrained, focused, and calibrated dark canvas. Visual density is strictly rationed; white space (or negative void) acts as an indicator of prestige and focus.

### Personality & Demeanor
- **Austere & Monastic:** Uncluttered, disciplined, and deliberately quiet. Only meaningful metrics earn the right to illuminate.
- **Architectural & Exact:** Calibrated down to single-pixel micro-borders, deliberate tracking, and tabular alignment.
- **Understated Excellence:** High-contrast accents are treated like precision complications on a mechanical chronograph—rare, hyper-functional, and singular.

### Emotional Target
The user should feel the stillness of an elite private training studio at 5:00 AM: focus, composure, premium mechanical craft, and scientific clarity.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around deep obsidian foundations, brushed platinum text layers, and a surgically disciplined kinetic accent.

### Palette Architecture
- **Obsidian Core (Canvas & Depths):**
  - Base Ground: `#08090B` (Infinite obsidian depth)
  - Surface Tier 1 (Cards, panels): `#0E1015`
  - Surface Tier 2 (Floating sheets, elevated modules): `#14171F`
  - Micro-Borders: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`
- **Champagne Platinum & Titanium (Text & Neutral UI):**
  - Primary Typography / Highlights: `#F1F5F9` (Warm platinum)
  - Secondary Typography / Structure: `#E2E8F0` (Champagne steel)
  - Muted Metadata / Sub-labels: `#71717A` (Titanium gray)
  - Deep Inactive / Tonal dividers: `#27272A`
- **Kinetic Volt (Micro-Accent):**
  - Primary Volt: `#D9FF00` (Electric amber-chartreuse)
  - Muted Volt Glow: `rgba(217, 255, 0, 0.12)` (Used exclusively for active telemetry beacons or state indicators)

### Accent Discipline Rules
- The kinetic volt accent must never cover more than 3% of any given viewport.
- Large background blocks of volt are strictly forbidden. It is reserved for:
  - Critical performance thresholds (PR broken, peak strain target met).
  - Single active telemetry status dots.
  - Micro-needles, active sparkline endpoints, and select primary action state bars.

## Typography

The typographical hierarchy strikes a deliberate balance between architectural form, high-density legibility, and horological precision.

- **Primary Display (`Space Grotesk`):** Provides a structured, mechanical nuance without brutalist heaviness. Used for high-level numbers, hero section titles, and macro-metrics.
- **Body & Structural Text (`Geist`):** Delivers neutral, developer-grade legibility with minimal visual noise, remaining razor-sharp across retina and OLED panels.
- **Instrument Telemetry & Labels (`JetBrains Mono`):** Applied to telemetry values, cadence units, timestamps, delta percentages, and technical sub-headers. Always set with open letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) and frequently transformed to uppercase for an instrument-readout aesthetic.
- **Numbers:** Telemetry values must always utilize tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent jitter during live rep and telemetry streaming.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is governed by rigorous column mathematics paired with expansive breathing room, evoking the quiet expanse of high-end editorial magazines and precision instrument monitors.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column fixed/max-width container (1360px max), with `2rem` gutters and `3rem` canvas margins. Modules occupy balanced fractional units (e.g., 4-col metrics, 8-col telemetry charts).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column fluid grid, `1.5rem` gutters, `2rem` canvas margins.
- **Mobile (≤ 767px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1.25rem` outer margin and `0.75rem` internal gutters.

### Spacing Philosophy
- Negative space is treated as an active luxury material. Components must never crowd borders.
- Padding inside metric cards is generous (minimum `1.5rem` on desktop, `1.25rem` on mobile).
- Vertical stack cadence follows an exact 4px/8px geometric progression: data groupings link via `space-xs` and `space-sm`, while modular systems separate via `space-xl` and `space-2xl`.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than relying on generic drop shadows, this design system establishes depth through **low-contrast micro-borders**, **tonal obsidian layering**, and **whisper-thin glassmorphic refraction**.

### Layering System
1. **Base Surface (Ground):** `#08090B` — The non-reflective abyss.
2. **Level 1 Container (Cards & Panels):** `#0E1015` backed with a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`. No shadow.
3. **Level 2 Layer (Overlays, Floating Control Ribbons, Active Modals):**
   - Background: `rgba(20, 23, 31, 0.85)`
   - Filter: `backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(140%)`
   - Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)`
   - Shadow: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`

### The Precision Micro-Border
Every card, tab, and surface edge utilizes an ultra-fine border (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`). This provides crisp edge delineation on deep black displays without creating harsh geometric cuts. When a card is hovered or focused, the border transitions seamlessly to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`—never to a bright white or electric color, preserving the quiet luxury ethos.

## Shapes

The shape system utilizes soft, architecturally measured radiuses (`roundedness: 1`), invoking the milled titanium finish of luxury chronographs and precision laboratory hardware.

### Radius Scaling
- **Micro Elements (Chips, Checkboxes, Inner Badges):** `4px` (`0.25rem`)
- **Containers & Interactive Surfaces (Cards, Inputs, Buttons):** `8px` (`0.5rem`)
- **Macro Layout Modules (Drawers, Modals, Full-bleed sheets):** `12px` (`0.75rem`)
- **Data Points & Status Beads:** `9999px` (Strictly reserved for small circular beacon indicators and telemetry nodes).

Pill shapes are intentionally avoided on primary buttons to maintain an architectural, technical demeanor.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic Action:** Platinum fill (`#F1F5F9`) with deep obsidian text (`#08090B`). Height: 44px. Font: `Geist` Medium, 14px. On hover: subtle scale transition (1.005) and shift to pure white (`#FFFFFF`). No neon background.
- **Secondary Ghost Action:** Transparent background with obsidian base, 1px micro-border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)`), text in `#E2E8F0`. Hover state elevates the border to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.22)` with background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`.
- **Volt Micro-Action (Rare):** Solid `#D9FF00` text with a 1px border of `rgba(217, 255, 0, 0.25)` over transparent black. Reserved solely for live session initiation or milestone lock-in.

### Telemetry Cards
- Constructed with `#0E1015` surface, 8px corner radius, and `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`.
- Internal architecture features an upper metadata row with an uppercase label in `JetBrains Mono` (`label-mono`), tracking at `0.08em`, color `#71717A`.
- Large numerical readout in `Space Grotesk` (`metric-display`) in `#F1F5F9` with unit markers set in smaller mono gray.
- Telemetry trend sparklines are drawn with 1.25px hairline curves in platinum `#E2E8F0`, with the trailing apex point illuminated by a 3px circle of `#D9FF00`.

### Chips & Telemetry Tags
- Minimalist pill-less form factor (4px radius).
- Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`.
- Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Typography: `label-mono` (11px), color `#E2E8F0`.
- Active State: Background `rgba(217, 255, 0, 0.08)`, border `rgba(217, 255, 0, 0.3)`, text `#D9FF00`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Height: 46px.
- Background: `#0B0C10`.
- Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Text: 14px `Geist`, `#F1F5F9`. Placeholder: `#71717A`.
- Focus State: Border transitions to titanium `#E2E8F0` with zero box-shadow glow. Focus is signaled by clinical contrast, not bright halos.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Size: 18px × 18px.
- Shape: 3px rounded rectangle for checkboxes; pure circle for radios.
- Idle State: `#0E1015` fill with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`.
- Selected State: Background `#0E1015`, border `#D9FF00`, with a single 6px solid `#D9FF00` core indicator.

### Chronometer & Set Lists
- Structured rows separated by hairline dividers (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`).
- Padding: 16px vertical, 0px horizontal within the container.
- Columns aligned via tabular mono: Set index, Load (kg/lbs), Rep count, Velocity/RPE, and Status.
- Completed sets feature a muted titanium strikethrough or dimming to `#71717A`, keeping the screen free of distracting success colors.
