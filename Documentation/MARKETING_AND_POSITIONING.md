# Kilo: Marketing Context & Positioning Guide

Use this context when creating landing page copy, waitlist copy, founder story copy, donation copy, early user messaging, and investor-facing summaries for **Kilo**.

---

## 1. What Kilo Is
Kilo is a **voice-first workout logger** and **AI workout tracking assistant** for people who train seriously but hate manually logging sets during workouts.

**Core Promise:**
Instead of stopping after every set to unlock a phone and type reps and weight into an app, the lifter holds a single button, speaks naturally (e.g., *"Bench press 80 kilos for 10"*, or simply *"90 for 8"* / *"same as last"*), and gets an instant haptic and earbud confirmation while their rest timer starts automatically.

---

## 2. Current Stage & Honesty Rules
- Kilo is in the early pre-launch / beta stage, built in public by a solo developer who lifts.
- Do **not** fabricate traction, waitlist numbers, testimonials, or investor claims.
- **Primary CTA**: Join the early waitlist with email.
- **Secondary CTA**: Optionally support indie development with a donation.

---

## 3. Target Audience
### Primary Audience
1. Serious lifters who already track workouts (progressive overload, 1RM, volume) but hate manual data entry between sets.
2. Gym-goers who know they should track workouts consistently but stop because typing mid-workout breaks their focus.

### Who Kilo Is Not For First
- Casual users who do not care about tracking sets/weights.
- People looking for a generic AI lifestyle coach, meal planner, or social network.

---

## 4. Core Problem & Product Solution
### The Problem
Logging workouts creates friction at the worst possible moment—right after a heavy set when hands are sweaty or chalky, heart rate is high, the gym is loud, and focus should be on training.

### The Kilo Solution (Actual Implemented Flows)
1. **Mode 1 — Live Interactive Workout (Zero-Touch Set-by-Set)**:
   - Hold-to-Talk Push-to-Talk gate ignores background gym noise and music.
   - Smart Context Memory remembers the active exercise so subsequent sets only need *"85 for 8"* or *"same weight, same reps"*.
   - Instant haptic buzz + optional earbud TTS receipt + auto 60-second rest timer.
2. **Mode 2 — Batch Workout Ingestion (Log Whole Workout in One Go)**:
   - Speak an entire completed routine in one clip (*"Bench press 3 sets of 10 at 80 kg, then Squat 100 kg for 5 reps"*).
   - Review the auto-grouped table preview and save in one tap.

---

## 5. Positioning & Copywriting Guidelines

### Good Positioning Examples
- *"Voice-first workout logging for lifters."*
- *"The fastest way to log your sets without typing."*
- *"Log your workout without breaking your workout."*
- *"Built for people who train seriously but hate manual tracking."*

### Avoid
- *"Your all-in-one wellness ecosystem"*
- *"The future of human performance intelligence"*
- Overly technical backend jargon in user-facing copy.

### One-Sentence Pitch
> Kilo is a voice-first workout logger for lifters who care about progressive overload but hate manually typing every set in the middle of training.
